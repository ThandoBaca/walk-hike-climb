West Coast – Beyond What You See

4 days, 3 nights. One journey through landscapes and stories that stretch far beyond what you see today. From the historic town of Clanwilliam, through the Cederberg mountain range and on to the coast — discover the many layers of the West Coast, its landscapes, its people and the stories held within them.

West Coast – Beyond What You See: 2-Day Tour

Explore the fascinating heritage, culture and coastal beauty of South Africa’s West Coast. Discover ancient rock art at Diepkloof Rock Shelter, unwind in the seaside town of Yzerfontein, and experience the rich San heritage of !Khwa ttu San Heritage Centre. A 2-day journey combining history, culture, nature and coastal relaxation.
