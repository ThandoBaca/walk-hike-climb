About Walk Hike Climb

Our Purpose & Guiding Ethos

Walk Hike Climb was created by Ian Naidoo (registered adventure site guide with the Department of Tourism & Certified South African Expert) and Diane Naidoo (accounting & NGO administration leader). Based in Goodwood, Cape Town, we turn outdoor exploration into meaningful, educational life encounters.

Our Core Mission

“To provide small-group, all-encompassing travel experiences for travelers interested in learning, celebrating, and preserving our region’s natural and cultural heritage while actively supporting local communities. We offer ethical, honest, and enjoyable encounters on every trail.”

The Four Pillars of WHC

  • Walk because you can — embracing wellness and accessibility.
  • Hike because you love it — deep respect for mountain terrain.
  • Climb because the mountain is there — courage and new summits.
  • Learn because you want to — not all classrooms have walls!

Trail Readiness

What to Prepare for Your Tour

To ensure a safe, enjoyable, and comfortable journey, please review our essential preparation checklist before joining an excursion.

Footwear & Attire

  • Broken-in hiking boots or trail runners with grip
  • Breathable moisture-wicking layers
  • Lightweight wind/rain jacket
  • Wide-brim hat or sun cap

Hydration & Snacks

  • Minimum 1.5L to 2L of water per person
  • Rehydration electrolyte packs
  • High-energy trail mix, dried fruit, or biltong
  • Light packed lunch for full-day tours

Sun & Safety

  • SPF 50+ sunscreen and lip balm
  • UV-rated sunglasses
  • Personal prescription medications
  • Fully charged mobile phone

Multi-Day & WOR

  • Comfortable 35L-45L daypack or duffel
  • Warm fleece for cool evenings
  • Journal or notebook for Walk of Reflection
  • Small travel towel & toiletries
