Helpful Details

Frequently Asked Booking Questions

Quick answers to common questions about small-group tours, payments, and weather policies.

How far in advance should we book?

For multi-day tours (such as the 4-Day West Coast journey or Walk of Reflection), we recommend booking at least 1 month in advance to secure wilderness lodging. Single-day hikes and city tours require confirmation 48 hours in advance.

What are the payment and deposit terms?

Tours are payable upon written confirmation of booking. We provide official bank transfer and secure card invoice links. Once payment is confirmed, your dates and guide are locked in exclusively.

Do you pick up from our hotel or Airbnb?

Yes. All of our guided day tours and multi-day expeditions include convenient, air-conditioned pick-up and drop-off at your accommodation within the Cape Town metropolitan area.

What happens if weather turns unsafe?

Safety is non-negotiable. If gale-force winds or severe rain hit Table Mountain, Ian will contact you early morning to reroute to a sheltered alternate route (such as lower forest trails or cultural city visits) or reschedule where possible.
