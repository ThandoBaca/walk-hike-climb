Generations on the Mountain

The Story Behind Walk Hike Climb

Walk Hike Climb was not formed in a corporate boardroom. It was born on the wind-swept ridgelines of Table Mountain, nurtured over decades of backcountry exploration, and built around a deep reverence for South Africa's cultural tapestry.

The Bicycle on the Slopes

It began with a ten-year-old boy pedalling his bicycle across the lower slopes of Table Mountain. Ian spent his childhood enchanted by the mountain's folds, memorizing granite crags, indigenous fynbos varieties, and the rich history that has shaped the Cape of Storms.

The "Landie" & Noddy the Colt

When Ian met Diane, they discovered a shared heartbeat for the wilderness. Armed with an old tent, their trusted Land Rover, and a beloved Colt 1100 nicknamed "Noddy", the couple spent weekends traversing forgotten gravel passes, mapping ancient trails from the Cederberg to False Bay.

A Family Tradition Passed Down

When their daughters Dominique and Juliet arrived, adventure did not pause—it flourished. Both girls experienced their first mountain hikes while still nestled in baby carriers, growing up immersed in campfire stories, bird calls, and living archaeological heritage.

"We don't offer crowded bus tours or rehearsed scripts. When you walk with us, you are walking as a personal guest of our family, discovering the sacred San rock shelters, hidden trails, and living stories that you will carry for a lifetime."

— Ian Naidoo, Lead Guide & Founder

Ethical & Conscious Travel

Our Guiding Philosophy

Every step we take is shaped by respect for nature, heritage, and genuine human connection.

Small Groups, True Connection

We strictly limit our tour groups to 6 to 10 guests. This ensures individual attention, unhurried pacing, and meaningful conversations that large bus tours can never offer.

Living San & Cultural Heritage

We work in deep respect with living indigenous communities and historical custodians. We interpret ancient rock art, slave history, and colonial narratives with cultural sensitivity and truth.

Safety Without Compromise

Table Mountain and Western Cape backcountry require respect. Ian is certified and carries comprehensive wilderness first-aid kits, satellite communications, and weather telemetry on every trek.

Leave No Trace Stewardship

We actively protect the delicate Cape Floral Kingdom. Our tours tread lightly, keep to sustainable pathways, pack out all waste, and instill conservation awareness in every visitor.
